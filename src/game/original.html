    How to play

    1. You'll see past trends, school and everyday life, the words people use and more. Pick how well you know each one, or the answer that fits you. Quizzes pop up now and then.
    2. The questions change to fit you, based on your answers. If you don't know much about one genre, it switches to questions from other genres.
    3. If you don't know or don't remember something, please honestly pick "Don't know" or "Not sure". It guesses better than if you force an answer.
    4. It names your age only once, at the very end. Tell it whether it was right, and it gets sharper for the next player.

    *Just for fun. The ages and answers you share are only added up as counts (nothing records whose age it was).
